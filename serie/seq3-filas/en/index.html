Sequence 3 of 4 · Queues

Ten tasks and go to sleep

A task queue for your agents, with recovery if something stops and a gate where work waits for your approval.

Watch the video ↓Open the inemaccbot guide →

An agent in the terminal does one thing at a time and only while you're watching. A queue solves that: each request becomes a saved task, picked up one at a time and resumed if the machine goes down. inemaccbot is a Telegram bot with this durable queue, running skills such as transcription, dubbing, explainer videos, courses, and reels. In multi-stage workflows, a human gate makes the bot stop and wait for your approval. This is where you go from doing the work to managing it.

The problem

One agent on its own gets lost

It does one thing at a time, and only while you're watching.

It stops, work is lost

If the internet goes down or the machine restarts, the work in progress disappears.

Ten requests collide

Simultaneous requests compete for the same folder and the same agent.

You stuck at the screen

Without a queue, someone has to keep sending the next request.

The queue

Durable queue: nothing gets lost

Each request becomes a task saved in a SQLite database.

Request on Telegram→Task saved→Picked up with a heartbeat→Running→Done or back in the queue

While it runs, the task sends a heartbeat. If the signal stops because the machine went down or the process froze, the task goes back in the queue and resumes. Before installing the bot, you can see the same idea in 30 lines with this repository's home-built queue:

mkdir -p fila/entrada
echo "Resuma em 5 linhas o que é uma fila de tarefas." > fila/entrada/01.md
echo "Liste 3 riscos de deixar um agente rodando sozinho." > fila/entrada/02.md
./ferramentas/fila-caseira.sh fila   # replies in fila/feito/

The gate

Automated, with you in control

Multi-stage workflows, with the state saved at each stage.

1

The bot writes the copy

The workflow's first stage, without spending anything expensive.

2

Then it stops at the gate

You read it, make any needed changes, and approve or reject it.

3

Only then does it continue

Avatar, download, and video run after you say okay.

You define the tasks, gates, and rules. The agents run through the queue, and you decide at the points that matter. From doing the work to managing it.

Practice

Exercises in this sequence

Do them in order. Each exercise has a "ready when" criterion: move on only when it is true.

  1. E1

    Map the queue on paper · 15 min

    Ready when: 10 tasks are listed, and at least two have a gate.

  2. E2

    A home-built queue in 30 lines · 30 min

    Ready when: the tasks are in fila/feito/ and the queue picked up where it left off after Ctrl+C.

  3. E3

    Install inemaccbot · 1 h

    Ready when: /ping replies on your Telegram.

  4. E4

    Ten tasks and go to sleep · 1 night

    Ready when: you checked every result and noted how many were right the first time.

  5. E5

    The gate · 30 min

    Ready when: you rejected or corrected at least one text before approving it.
